About

Cool facts about me

I am a passionate software engineer.

I'm comfortable around and interested by distributed systems, API design, automated testing tools, machine learning algorithms, system administration, recruiting tech profiles and laws (regulations, public sector transparency, data privacy). I have been involved at different levels in a lot of sectors: Internet service provider, car rental, sea rescue operations, customer service, fraud detection, inspiring quotes for teenagers, gaming servers, equity crowdfunding, mobility services... Did I mention that I'm French? Sorry, I don't live in Paris: I live in Rouen, Normandy. You should visit both.

I studied at the INSA Rouen and the University of Rouen with majors in software engineering, machine learning and data mining. My day job is spent on open-source projects and open data resources. I also tried to wakeboard, but I'd rather not discuss that.

I love to learn.

I'm a Senior Software Engineer at beta.gouv.fr, working for Choisir le service public, the hiring platform of the French public administration: 60k job offers and 13M visitors per year. I'm also an independent financial advisor (CGPI) at Augusti Conseil, registered as a CIF with the ORIAS (n° 26011368) and paid by fees only, with no commissions. I help executives, business owners and liberal professions with tax optimisation, investments, retirement and estate planning.

Previously, I worked on the French national access point to transport data, at the Canadian Digital Service in the federal government of Canada, where I was Tech Lead for GC Notify, at the French maritime affairs administration on search and rescue at sea, and at Etalab, in charge of the French government data policy and data-driven transformation for public administrations.

Teaching & mentoring

Sharing what I know

Sciences Po

I taught classes about web technologies and numerical arts at Sciences Po Paris and Reims. Topics covered are: algorithms, HTML, CSS, Internet architecture, history of the Web, writing small programs and web pages.

HEC Paris

I mentored students in the Polytechnique-HEC entrepreneurship program. I helped them navigate the tech sector, how to start a startup, how to hire talents, understand technologies and costs.

Burgundy University

I taught classes about relational and NoSQL databases and mentored end-of-year projects related to real-time transport systems.

INSA Rouen

I taught classes about NoSQL databases versus relational databases and practicals session in networking about packet switching, bridges, VLANs, DNS and DHCP to engineering students in their fourth year.

OpenClassrooms

I mentored online students in order to help them obtain certifications and diplomas. I helped them learn the following technologies: HTML5, CSS3, PHP, relational databases, object oriented design, web server administration and basic networking.

Drivy

I helped non-technical employees discover databases and learn some bits of SQL to help them get the data they need to answer questions. This was also an opportunity to show what are the various components behind a typical web application.
